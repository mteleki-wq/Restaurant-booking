STEP 10 — ACQUISITION AUTHORISATION

Final acquisition form & asset handover

This form automatically imports the final selected asset, matching supplier, researched price, specifications, warranty, maintenance, and management decision from your completed investigation and report. Only genuinely missing approval, quotation, payment and handover details require confirmation. AI recommendations never count as management approval.

Purchase control: The tool will only label the form Authorised for Purchase when the recorded management decision is an approval, the approving manager is identified, an approval date is recorded, approval conditions are confirmed resolved, and the essential acquisition fields are completed. No supplier order is sent by this tool.

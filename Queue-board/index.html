Questions

Good to know.

Do guests need to download an app?+
No. They scan the QR code with their phone’s camera and the queue opens in their browser. Nothing to install and no account to create.
Do you collect guests’ phone numbers?+
No. Guests give a first name and party size only. No phone number, no email, so there’s no customer data for you to look after.
Does it work with my POS?+
Yes. Scan & Queue runs on its own, so it sits happily alongside whatever POS you already use. There’s nothing to connect or replace.
What do I need at the restaurant?+
An internet connection, any iPad, tablet or phone for the queue board, and the printed QR sign for your door. That’s it.
What about guests without a smartphone?+
Your staff add them to the queue by name on the board, so they keep their place in line like everyone else.
How is the wait time worked out?+
You set a starting guess of minutes per party. As you seat people, the estimate adjusts to how fast your restaurant really turns tables, so guests get a realistic wait.
Can I close the queue when we’re full for the night?+
Yes, one tap. New guests who scan see that the queue is closed, and anyone already waiting keeps their place.
Is there a contract?+
No. It’s month to month and you can cancel any time. Pay yearly instead and you get two months free.

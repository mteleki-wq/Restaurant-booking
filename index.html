Questions

Straight answers.

Which one is right for my business?

Restaurants, cafés and bars: Restaurant Deluxe. Spas, salons, clinics and other appointment businesses: Service Deluxe. Anything else: a custom website built from the same pieces. Not sure? Ask our assistant or book a free Zoom chat.

Do you take a commission on bookings or orders?

No. You pay one flat monthly price and every sale stays yours.

Can I change the website myself?

Yes. Tap any words or photo to change them, right in your browser — no developer needed. We’re here if you’d like a hand.

How much does a custom website cost?

It depends on what your business needs, so we quote after a free Zoom chat. Restaurant Deluxe starts from $68 a month (AUD) and Service Deluxe from $39 a month + GST, both with 14 days free.

Can the AI assistant get things wrong?

It answers only from the information you give it and says when it isn’t sure, offering to pass the question to you. Like any AI it can occasionally make a mistake, so it’s clearly labelled as an AI assistant on your website.

Who owns my customer list?

You do. Bookings, enquiries and email subscribers are your customers, kept in your own list.

Do you set up my domain and hosting?

Yes — we connect your own web address and get you live, and we stay with you after launch.

Bookings + walk-ins

Never give away a booked table.

Guests book straight with you — choosing a sitting and an exact arrival time that fits your capacity. Today’s bookings sit on the front counter, right next to the queue.

  • Booked tables are keptThey show as reserved and aren’t offered to walk-ins.
  • A warning before it’s too lateStaff are told if seating a walk-in would leave too few seats for bookings due soon.
  • No-shows freed in one tapThe table goes straight back to the waitlist. Big parties can have joined tables, like 5 + 6.
  • Large parties protectedParties of 8 or more can confirm with a secure Stripe card guarantee — cards are never stored by us.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you take a commission on orders or bookings?

No. You pay one flat monthly fee and every sale stays yours.

Do guests need an app, or pay on their phone?

No app and no account. Guests scan the table QR code and order; the order goes to the restaurant and the bill is paid at the counter or table, as usual.

Does it replace our POS?

No. Restaurant Deluxe runs alongside your existing POS. It handles ordering, the kitchen and bar flow, bookings, the queue and your website.

Who receives the Email Studio emails?

Only guests who ticked “Keep me posted” when they booked online and are still subscribed. Every email carries your address and its own unsubscribe link. Email Studio comes with the Deluxe and Complete plans.

Can we change the menu and prices ourselves?

Yes — dishes, descriptions, photos, prices, modifiers and availability. Mark a dish sold out and it disappears from the website and the table menu straight away.

Do bookings and walk-ins work together?

Yes. Today’s bookings show on the front counter next to the waitlist; booked tables are kept, staff are warned before giving away seats a booking will need, and no-shows go back to the queue in one tap.

What does the free trial include?

For 14 days we connect your restaurant at no cost so you can try it with your own team. Loading your menu, photos and add-ons isn’t part of the free trial — choose the Quick Start if you’d like us to do it. Our first 5 restaurants get 28 days with setup done for them.

What hardware do we need?

None from us — it runs in a web browser on the tablets, phones and computers you already have. Compatible printers you already own can be connected.

TDC quick guide

Centre the small flywheel notch in the inspection opening.
Then confirm the cylinder is on the compression stroke.

Correct compression TDC

Engine cold. Select 6th gear and rotate only in the normal forward direction.
Watch the valves. The intake valves must have opened and then fully closed before compression TDC.
Bring the TDC notch to the centre of the inspection opening, if using the crank-sensor window.
Both intake and exhaust rockers on that cylinder must be unloaded/free. If not, rotate to the next TDC.
The crankshaft reaches TDC twice in a four-stroke cycle. Do not adjust at overlap TDC. Both valve groups must be closed/free.

Valve-clearance procedure

A cleaner step-by-step layout for quick reading on your phone while you work.

1

Prepare the bike

Start here

Set up the bike and your work area before removing anything.

  • Engine must be cold, ideally below 35 C.
  • Lay out your tools, rags, feeler gauges and catch tray.
  • Protect painted surfaces around the cylinder head.
  • Disconnect the upper ignition-coil connector.
Cold engine onlyTray for small oil drips
2

Remove access parts

Open access

Take off only the parts needed to reach the rocker gear and ease engine rotation.

  • Remove the outer cover / brackets for access.
  • Remove the upper/main spark plug using the 14 mm thin-wall socket.
  • The lower secondary spark plug can stay in place.
  • Remove the valve cover carefully and mind the gasket.
Spark plug: 12 Nm on refitCover fasteners: see torque table
3

Find compression TDC

Most important step

Get the cylinder you are adjusting to compression TDC, not overlap TDC.

  • Select 6th gear and roll the bike slowly forward only.
  • Watch the intake valves: they must open and then fully close.
  • If using the inspection opening, centre the small TDC notch.
  • Check that both rockers are free on that cylinder.
Both rockers free = correct TDCIf tight, rotate one full turn to next TDC
4

Measure the clearance

Check first

BMW specifies the same cold range for both intake and exhaust.

  • Intake: 0.20-0.30 mm
  • Exhaust: 0.20-0.30 mm
  • A good working target is 0.25 mm.
  • Insert the blade and feel for a light, even drag.
0.20 mm = easy0.25 mm = light drag0.30 mm = tight / may not pass
5

Adjust if needed

10 mm + Allen key

Use the locknut and centre adjuster carefully — small movements make a difference.

  • Loosen the 10 mm locknut.
  • Turn the centre hex adjuster very slightly.
  • Set the clearance so the chosen blade has light drag.
  • Do not let the adjuster move while final tightening.
Use the snug-fitting Allen keyTiny movement = noticeable change
6

Tighten and recheck

Torque critical

This is the stage where the setting can shift, so always confirm it again.

  • Hold the centre adjuster steady.
  • Tighten the locknut to 8 Nm.
  • Measure again immediately after tightening.
  • Repeat until the setting stays correct.
Locknut: 8 NmRecheck every valve after torque
7

Repeat on the other cylinder

Same method

Once one cylinder is complete, repeat the whole sequence on the opposite side.

  • Rotate the engine again until the other cylinder reaches compression TDC.
  • Confirm the rockers on that side are also free.
  • Measure, adjust and torque the remaining valves.
Same specs on both sidesKeep recording your measured values
8

Reassemble and final check

Finish the job

Put everything back carefully, then do a calm final inspection before riding.

  • Seat the valve-cover gasket properly.
  • Torque the valve cover, sensor and spark plug using the torque matrix.
  • Reconnect ignition components and refit covers / brackets.
  • Start the engine and check for leaks or abnormal persistent noise.
Valve cover: 10 NmCrank sensor: 10 NmSpark plug: 12 Nm

Oil service modules

Keep these closed while you are working on the valves. Open only the service you need.

Engine oil + filter
Oil quantity4.0 Lwith filter change
Oil specificationSAE 15W-50API SJ / JASO MA2
Drain plug28 NmM16 x 1.5
Oil filter11 Nmoil sealing ring first

Oil & parts

  • 4.0 L engine oil for a filter change.
  • SAE 15W-50, API SJ / JASO MA2. BMW recommends ADVANTEC Pro.
  • New oil filter. BMW filter reference commonly listed: 11 42 7 673 541.
  • New engine drain-plug sealing ring: A16 x 20 AL / BMW 07 11 9 963 252.

Tools

  • 8 mm Allen/hex for the engine drain plug.
  • 76 mm x 12-flute oil-filter cup wrench for the OEM-style filter.
  • 3/8-inch ratchet and suitable extensions.
  • Torque wrench that accurately covers 11-28 Nm.
  • Drain pan of at least 5 L, funnel, gloves and rags.
  • BMW/on-board oil-filler-cap tool if required for your cap.

Torque

PartDetailTorque
Engine oil drain plugM16 x 1.5, fit new A16 x 20 sealing ring28 Nm
Oil filterLubricate the filter sealing ring with clean engine oil before installation11 Nm

Procedure

Warm the oil, then switch off. A short ride makes the oil flow more easily. Secure the motorcycle safely before working underneath it.
Open the filler first. Confirm the filler cap can be removed before draining the engine.
Drain the engine oil. Place the drain pan underneath, remove the M16 drain plug with the 8 mm Allen and allow the oil to drain fully.
Remove the oil filter. Use the 76 mm x 12-flute filter wrench. Make sure the old rubber seal comes off with the old filter.
Install the new filter. Lightly oil the new filter seal and torque the filter to 11 Nm.
Install the drain plug. Fit a new A16 x 20 sealing ring and torque the drain plug to 28 Nm.
Refill. Add approximately 4.0 L of the specified 15W-50 oil for a filter change. Do not intentionally overfill.
Final check. Refit the filler cap, run the engine briefly, stop it, inspect for leaks and verify the oil level using the BMW rider-manual procedure.
Important: never work under an R18 supported by improvised blocks or a car jack alone. The motorcycle is very heavy; use stable, purpose-designed support if lifting is required.
Reference: BMW rider manual engine-oil specification/capacity; R18 repair torque table for drain plug and oil filter; R18 owner documentation for the 8 mm drain tool and 76 mm x 12-flute filter wrench.
Gearbox / transmission oil
Oil quantity1.15 L1150 ml
Oil specificationSAE 70W-80FUCHS Titan EG 4218 / BMW spec
Drain plug20 NmM14 x 1.5
Filler plug30 NmM18 x 1.5

Oil & parts

  • 1.15 L transmission oil.
  • SAE 70W-80; BMW manual lists FUCHS Titan EG 4218.
  • Use fresh sealing rings on the fill and drain plugs.
  • The filler sealing ring is listed as A18 x 22 AL.

Tools

  • 19 mm hex socket with short extension for the drain plug.
  • 8 mm Allen/hex for the filler plug.
  • T25 Torx for the access cover.
  • Small flat screwdriver for the connector clip if access requires it.
  • Torque wrench covering 20-30 Nm.
  • Drain pan of at least 1.5 L and an oil pump / bottle with flexible hose.

Torque

PartDetailTorque
Transmission drain plugM14 x 1.520 Nm
Transmission filler plugM18 x 1.530 Nm

Procedure

Prepare the bike. A short ride can warm the gearbox oil. Park securely and position a drain pan under the transmission.
Remove the access cover. Use the T25 Torx and, where fitted, release the connector carefully with a small flat screwdriver.
Loosen the filler plug first. Use the 8 mm Allen. This confirms you can refill the gearbox before you drain it.
Drain the oil. Use the 19 mm hex socket on the drain plug and let the gearbox drain fully. Keep the nearby cable clear of the tool.
Refit the drain plug. Fit a fresh sealing ring, start the plug by hand and torque it to 20 Nm.
Refill the gearbox. Pump in 1.15 L of SAE 70W-80 through the filler opening.
Refit the filler plug. Fit a fresh sealing ring and torque the filler plug to 30 Nm.
Finish. Clean away spilled oil, reconnect anything removed, refit the cover and inspect both plugs for leaks after a short ride.
Torque note: some older community worksheets list 28 Nm for both transmission plugs. This companion uses the R18 repair torque table values: 20 Nm drain and 30 Nm filler.
Reference: BMW rider manual for SAE 70W-80 specification; R1800Wiki transmission procedure for 1.15 L and tools; R18 repair torque table for M14/M18 plug torques.
Rear final-drive oil
Oil quantity210 ml0.21 L
Oil specificationSAE 70W-80BMW Hypoid Axle Oil G3 / equivalent BMW spec
Drain plug20 NmM12 x 1
Filler plug25 NmM12 x 1.5

Oil & parts

  • 210 ml rear final-drive oil.
  • BMW specifies SAE 70W-80; BMW Hypoid Axle Oil G3 is the commonly supplied BMW product.
  • Fit a new sealing washer to the filler plug.
  • Inspect the drain-plug O-ring and replace it if damaged, flattened or leaking.

Tools

  • 6 mm Allen/hex for the filler plug.
  • T45 Torx for the magnetic drain plug.
  • Torque wrench covering 20-25 Nm.
  • Small drain pan.
  • Measuring jug / large syringe / oil pump with hose for accurately delivering 210 ml.
  • Cardboard or absorbent protection to keep oil off the rear tyre.

Torque

PartDetailTorque
Final-drive drain plugM12 x 1, magnetic plug20 Nm
Final-drive filler plugM12 x 1.5, new sealing washer recommended25 Nm

Procedure

Prepare. Secure the motorcycle and protect the rear tyre and wheel from any oil spill.
Loosen the filler first. Use the 6 mm Allen. Never drain the final drive until you know the filler plug will open.
Drain. Place the pan below and remove the magnetic drain plug with the T45 Torx.
Inspect the magnetic plug. Wipe it clean. A light paste/fine residue can occur; larger chips or pieces deserve professional inspection.
Refit the drain plug. Inspect the O-ring and torque the plug to 20 Nm.
Measure exactly 210 ml. Use a syringe, measuring jug or oil pump so the quantity is controlled.
Fill. Add the 210 ml of BMW-spec SAE 70W-80 final-drive oil through the filler opening.
Close and check. Fit a new filler sealing washer, torque the filler plug to 25 Nm, clean the housing and inspect for leaks after riding.
Keep oil off the tyre and brake components. If any gear oil reaches the tyre tread or brake disc/pads, clean or service the affected component properly before riding.
Reference: BMW rider manual for rear-axle oil specification; R1800Wiki final-drive procedure for 210 ml, 6 mm Allen and T45 tools; BMW-derived R18 torque table for 20/25 Nm.

QUICK PROCEDURE
  1. Engine cold, max 35 C. Protect covers and place a small tray below.
  2. Remove outer cover/brackets and upper/main spark plug. Lower plug can remain.
  3. Select 6th gear. No jack: slowly roll bike forward to rotate engine.
  4. Find compression TDC: intake has closed, TDC notch centred if using inspection window, both rocker groups free.
  5. Measure all valves on that cylinder. BMW range is 0.20-0.30 mm intake and exhaust.
  6. If adjusting: loosen 10 mm locknut, set centre screw gently; 0.25 mm with light drag is a practical midpoint.
  7. Hold adjuster, tighten locknut to 8 Nm, then recheck clearance. Repeat all four valves.
  8. Move to compression TDC on other cylinder, repeat, reassemble and torque fasteners.
TDC caution: the crank reaches TDC twice. Adjust only on compression TDC with both intake and exhaust rockers unloaded/free. Nearby coil/cover M6 fasteners may be 8 or 10 Nm; identify the exact fastener before tightening. Do not add threadlocker unless BMW specifies it.

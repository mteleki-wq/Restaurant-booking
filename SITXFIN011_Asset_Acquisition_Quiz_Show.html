🏆 Asset Acquisition Quiz Show

Round 2 warm-up • ~30–35 min

Ready to challenge the management team?

24 interactive questions in 3 rounds. Split the class into up to 4 teams. Discuss each question, choose an answer on the big screen, reveal the explanation and automatically award a point to the selected team for each correct answer.

Suggested timing: 3 minutes for rules, 8 questions × 1 minute in Round 1, 8 × 1.2 minutes in Round 2, 8 × 1.3 minutes in Round 3, plus 3–4 minutes for a final debrief. Approximately 30–35 minutes.

No accounts, internet connection or API keys required. Use a projector or classroom display. Keyboard: 1–4 select answer, Space reveal, → next question, F full screen.

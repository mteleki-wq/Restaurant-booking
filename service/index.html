Good to know

Questions

When should I arrive?

About ten minutes early so you can settle in and fill in a short health form.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes — use the link in your confirmation email up to 24 hours before. After that, please give us a call.

Do you offer health fund rebates?

Remedial massage with our qualified remedial therapists can be claimed on the spot with most health funds.

Can I choose my therapist?

Of course. Pick a name when you book, or choose “anyone available” to see the most times.

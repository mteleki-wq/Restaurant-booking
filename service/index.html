Questions

Straight answers.

Do my clients need an app or an account?

No. The booking page opens in any phone or computer browser. Clients enter their name, email and mobile, and that's it.

Can it handle rooms as well as people?

Yes. Each service can need a practitioner, a room, or both. A time is only offered when everything it needs is free, and the system won't book the same room or person twice.

Can clients cancel themselves?

Yes, from the link in their confirmation email, up to the cut-off you set (for example 24 hours before). After that they're asked to call you.

Do you send SMS reminders or take payments?

Not at the moment. Reminders are sent by email, and payment happens at your business as usual. There's no commission or booking fee on anything.

Is it a practice-management system?

No — it's bookings, your calendar and your website. It doesn't hold clinical notes or process health-fund or Medicare claims, so it sits happily alongside the systems you already use.

How much is the website setup?

It's optional and one-off. Quick connect is $40 + GST: we connect the website to your domain and you fill it in yourself. Done for you is $250 + GST: we load your words and photos. Either way, it's yours to edit. Bookings need no setup fee at all.

What happens after the 14 days?

If you'd like to keep going, choose a plan. If not, simply stop — there's nothing to cancel and no card needed to start.

Questions

How long is a date held?

A hold lasts 7 days. It becomes a confirmed booking once your deposit is paid. If another couple or company asks for the same date, we contact you first.

How accurate is the estimate?

It uses our current room hire, package prices and minimum spends for the day you choose. Final pricing is confirmed in writing after your meeting.

Why do some days cost more?

Saturdays carry the highest room hire and minimum spend. Sundays add a 10% surcharge on food and drinks, and public holidays add 15% to cover staff penalty rates.

What is the deposit?

25% of the estimated total, with a minimum of $1,000. It comes off your final invoice.

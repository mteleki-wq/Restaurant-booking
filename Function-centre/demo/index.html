Questions

How long is a date held?

A hold lasts 7 days. It becomes a confirmed booking once your deposit is paid. If someone else asks for the same date, we contact you first.

How accurate is the estimate?

It uses our current room hire, package prices and minimum spends for the day you choose. Final pricing is confirmed in writing after your consultation.

Why do some days cost more?

Saturdays carry the highest room hire and minimum spend. Sundays add a 10% surcharge on food and drinks, and public holidays add 15% to cover staff penalty rates.

Can we bring our own decorator or cake?

Yes. Our in-house team is optional, and we will work with your suppliers on bump-in times.

What is the deposit?

25% of the estimated total, with a minimum of $1,000. It comes off your final invoice.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is the estimate a firm quote?

No — it's a guide based on your own prices and rules for that day, room and package. Your coordinator confirms the final quote, usually at the consultation. The page says so clearly.

How are weekends and public holidays priced?

You set the rules: room hire and minimum spend for each room and session, and surcharges for Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. NSW public holidays are marked on the calendar and priced in automatically.

What happens when someone holds a date?

The date shows as "on hold" for that room and session, and you get the details with their estimate and chosen look. Holds last 7 days by default. You confirm, release or follow up from your venue view.

Can guests pay the deposit online?

Yes, through Stripe. We connect it to your Stripe account as part of setup, so the money goes straight to you. If you'd rather take deposits your usual way, guests can hold and book a meeting without paying online.

What does Ava know, and can she get it wrong?

Ava answers from your website content and the notes you give her, and uses your pricing rules for estimates. She's an AI assistant, so the chat says answers can be wrong and your coordinator confirms prices and dates. When she can't help, she takes a message for your team.

Do you charge commission?

Never. One flat monthly price, no percentage on any booking or deposit.

Is Aurelle a real venue?

No — Aurelle Function Centre is our demo venue, so you can try everything as a guest would. Nothing is charged and no real dates are held.

What happens after the 14 days?

If you'd like to keep going, choose a plan. If not, simply stop — there's nothing to cancel.

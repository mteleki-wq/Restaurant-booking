Questions

Straight answers.

How does it know how long the drive is?

By default it estimates the drive from the distance between suburbs, your average speed and a parking allowance you set — free, and close enough for local work. If you'd like real drive times with typical traffic, add your own Google Maps key; each pair of suburbs is looked up once and saved, so the cost stays very small.

I'm a one-person business. Is it for me?

Yes. With one tradie it simply plans your own day — you'll stop double-booking yourself across town. Add people later if you grow.

What if a job runs over?

Each job has a time bracket (say 1 to 1.5 hours) and the longer time is kept free. On the run sheet you can move a job to another tradie in one tap — it checks they can make it, travel included.

Can customers pay when they book?

Card payments at booking are coming soon. When they're switched on, it's your choice. You can take a call-out fee at booking (taken off the bill), an emergency fee, and a deposit when a quote is accepted. Payments go through Stripe into your own account. Or switch it off and take payment on the day.

Do my customers need an app or an account?

No. The booking page opens in any phone browser. They enter their address, name, mobile and email.

Does it do invoicing or job notes?

Not yet — it's bookings, scheduling, quotes and your website. Keep using your accounting software for invoices; Trade Deluxe sits alongside it.

Restaurant Deluxe

Restaurant website, bookings, table ordering and CRM

A complete system for independent restaurants: a website the owner edits, direct bookings, QR ordering from every table and a guest CRM with email marketing.

  • Bookings by sitting and exact arrival time, checked against the venue’s capacity, with a Stripe card guarantee for parties of eight or more.
  • QR table ordering with dietary filters, modifiers and allergy notes, routed to kitchen and bar displays. Guests settle at the counter or table.
  • Open-table tracking, cash and card settlement, X and Z readings and Excel-compatible shift export.
  • Guest CRM with segments, AI-drafted campaigns and a Spam Act–compliant unsubscribe, plus “Alex”, an AI assistant trained on the product.

Service Deluxe

Appointment booking and websites for service businesses

The same system rebuilt for day spas, dentists, doctors and salons. Clients pick a time and a named practitioner; the system checks every practitioner and treatment room before it offers a slot.

  • Multi-resource scheduling: a service can require a practitioner, a room, or both — so a slot only appears when everything it needs is free. A harder problem than a restaurant table.
  • Owner-editable website alongside it, with looks for different trades and an AI assistant on the premium plan whose knowledge the owner writes themselves.
  • Runs on Supabase alone, with no automation subscription, so a small clinic carries no cost beyond the platform.
  • Packaged as a product: free trial, two monthly plans, an optional setup service, and enquiries landing in the CRM as leads.

Built for the classroom

Over the past year I’ve turned the same tools on teaching. Students get more practice than a worksheet allows, and they leave able to use what employers are already adopting.

Interactive role-plays

A guest complaint at the pass, a dietary request, a function enquiry, a difficult client call. Students work through the scenario in the browser, make the decisions and see where each one leads — as many times as they need, without waiting for a partner or a class slot.

Practice quizzes and revision

Question sets built around the unit actually being delivered, which students can repeat before class and before assessment. Particularly useful for cohorts working in a second language, who can go at their own pace.

Costing, planning and campaign exercises

Menu costing, budgets, rosters and marketing plans built on real figures rather than textbook ones, so the numbers behave the way they do in a workplace and students can test a decision before they defend it.

Using AI properly, as a job skill

Students learn to brief a tool, check what comes back against the source, and declare where they used it. What employers are short of is judgement, not output — so that is what gets taught and practised.

On assessment: these are learning and practice materials. Assessment is conducted using the RTO’s own validated tools, and students are taught clearly where AI use is and isn’t appropriate in work they submit.

Behind the builds

The skills these projects rely on are the ones employers now ask graduates about.

Service design

Mapping the guest journey from first click to the kitchen pass, then removing the steps that cause errors.

Customer data and CRM

Guest profiles, segments, visit history and targeted campaigns that respect opt-outs.

AI assistants

Jotform AI agents and n8n concierges with knowledge bases and guardrails against invented claims.

Workflow automation

n8n workflows connecting forms, databases and Gmail for confirmations, reminders and alerts.

Compliance and risk

Spam Act, Privacy Act, Australian Consumer Law, card-payment handling and venue data separation.

Testing and improvement

Live-service testing, fault logging and fixes released quickly, the same cycle taught in continuous improvement.

Building with AI

Claude for architecture, code and iteration; ChatGPT for research, competitor and pricing analysis. The judgement about what to build stays with me.

Pricing and packaging

Trials, plan tiers, setup fees and a lead funnel — decided and tested on real products, not modelled on paper.

How the work gets done: every platform on this page was designed and built by me, with AI as the development partner rather than the author — Claude for architecture, code and iteration, ChatGPT for research, competitor analysis and content. Deciding what to build, how the data should be modelled and what a venue actually needs during service is the part that cannot be delegated, and it is the part worth teaching.

Restaurant Deluxe

Restaurant website, bookings, table ordering and CRM

A complete system for independent restaurants: a website the owner edits, direct bookings, QR ordering from every table and a guest CRM with email marketing.

  • Bookings by sitting and exact arrival time, checked against the venue’s capacity, with a Stripe card guarantee for parties of eight or more.
  • QR table ordering with dietary filters, modifiers and allergy notes, routed to kitchen and bar displays. Guests settle at the counter or table.
  • Open-table tracking, cash and card settlement, X and Z readings and Excel-compatible shift export.
  • Guest CRM with segments, AI-drafted campaigns and a Spam Act–compliant unsubscribe, plus “Alex”, an AI assistant trained on the product.

Behind the builds

The skills these projects rely on are the ones employers now ask graduates about.

Service design

Mapping the guest journey from first click to the kitchen pass, then removing the steps that cause errors.

Customer data and CRM

Guest profiles, segments, visit history and targeted campaigns that respect opt-outs.

AI assistants

Jotform AI agents and n8n concierges with knowledge bases and guardrails against invented claims.

Workflow automation

Compliance and risk

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You click, drag and type. If you can use a phone, you can build your website. And if you would rather we set it up for you, just ask.

How much does it cost?

Building and publishing one website is free. Platinum (bookings + AI assistant, no badge) is $49 a month + GST — or $29 a month + GST if you already use Restaurant, Service, Trade or Function Deluxe. The Shop has no monthly fee: 1.5% per sale plus Stripe’s normal fees. Want us to build it? Setup is $250 + GST, or $400 + GST with bookings, the AI assistant and shop set up too.

Will my website look good on phones?

Yes. Your phone layout is created automatically from your desktop page, in the right reading order. You can switch to Phone view at any time to fine-tune it, or hide items on phones.

Can I connect the booking system I already use?

Yes, with Platinum. Restaurant Deluxe, Service Deluxe and Trade Deluxe connect in a couple of clicks — or paste the link of any other online booking page (it must start with https://).

How does the AI assistant know about my business?

It reads the words on your own website — your menu, services, prices and opening hours. When you update your website and publish, the assistant knows the new information too.

How do I get paid from the Shop?

You connect your own Stripe account. Customers pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay and the money goes into your Stripe account, then to your bank. Stripe’s normal fees apply, plus a small Webescapade fee of 1.5% per sale.

Can I import my menu, price list or resume?

Yes. Drop in a PDF or Word file, or paste the text. Menus and price lists become a menu section, or Shop products if you have the Shop add-on. With Platinum, your resume becomes a complete website: intro, about, experience, skills, education and contact. Everything is read on your own device, and you check it before it goes on your page.

I use Restaurant Deluxe. Can my website show my live menu?

Yes. Add the Live menu section and type your venue code. Your website shows the same menu guests order from when they scan the QR code at the table, with photos, prices, dietary tags and sold-out dishes. Change anything in the Restaurant Deluxe Menu Manager and your website updates by itself, with no need to publish again.

Can I use my own photos?

Of course. Upload them from your phone or computer, then drag them where you want them. Choose the focus point so faces and dishes stay in the frame.

What is coming next?

A CRM to see all your customers, bookings and orders in one place, then Email Studio so you can send beautiful emails to your own customers. Join the early-access list to be first.

A few things to know.

Is the estimate a fixed moving price?

No. It is a planning range based on approximate volume, driving distance and the selected access details. Inventory, truck size, traffic, tolls, specialist items and actual access may change the final quote.

What does a quotation visit book?

An appointment to inspect and discuss your move. It does not reserve a moving date, truck or crew. These are confirmed directly with the removalist.

Can it calculate the distance between properties?

You can enter driving kilometres yourself or calculate an approximate route between the suburbs/postcodes. Suburb-based routing is not a truck-access or live-traffic guarantee.

Do stairs and apartments affect the estimate?

Yes. Add floors, lift access and carrying distance at both ends. Lift bookings, parking restrictions and building rules need confirmation with the removalist.

What is excluded?

Unless the business explicitly includes them, tolls, overnight stays, storage, ferry costs, insurance upgrades, pianos, pool tables and specialist handling need a separate written quote.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is the moving estimate a final quote?

No. It is an indicative range using the business’s configured rates and customer inputs. Room counts approximate volume. Inventory, truck size, access, traffic, tolls and specialist items need confirmation.

Can it estimate distance automatically?

Customers can enter driving kilometres or request an approximate route between suburbs/postcodes using OpenStreetMap and OSRM. It is not live traffic or a truck-access guarantee. Manual distance entry remains available if routing is unavailable.

Does booking reserve moving day?

No. Online appointment slots reserve quotation visits only. Customers may request a moving date, and the removalist confirms the truck, crew and moving date directly.

Can each removalist edit the chatbot?

Yes. The signed-in Website editor has an on/off switch, a chatbot name and an extra public information box. Live answers use that business’s published details; private customer records are not supplied to the assistant.

What about CRM and Email Studio?

Basic customer records are included now. Advanced follow-ups, campaigns and Email Studio are planned upgrades. Launch dates and upgrade prices are to be confirmed.

A few things to know.

Is the online estimate a final quote?

No. It gives you a starting budget. The painter checks measurements, surface preparation, access and finishes before confirming the price.

Am I booking the painting job?

The initial appointment is a site visit for a quote. Painting work is scheduled once the scope, price and duration are agreed.

Does the calendar allow for travel?

It checks the painter’s working hours and existing bookings, with a travel allowance between suburbs and a parking buffer. The time is checked again when the booking is submitted.

Will the colour look exactly like the screen?

The illustrations provide inspiration. Lighting, screens and paint finishes change appearance. Always test a physical paint sample.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does it prevent double bookings?

It checks the selected painter’s hours, time off, existing appointments and travel allowance, then checks the slot again on the server at confirmation. Telephone bookings must also be entered into the system.

Can I use it as a one-person business?

Yes. Start with one painter, set your available days and hours, and use the same booking and quote tools as a team.

Does the estimate replace my final quote?

No. It is an indicative budget based on the customer’s dimensions and your rates. You inspect the property to confirm preparation, access, finishes and the final written price.

Can it book a whole multi-day painting project?

The initial online booking is a quote visit. Multi-day painting projects need manual planning of each work day. Automatic multi-day reservations and card payments are not included in this release.

Do customers need an app or login?

No. Customers use the website, estimate and booking form in their browser. The private management dashboard requires an authorised staff login.

Are CRM and Email Studio included?

A basic customer view is available now, bringing together the records returned by the booking and quote tools. Advanced CRM follow-ups, email campaigns and Email Studio are planned upgrades. Timing and pricing will be confirmed before release.

Follow-up journey

Choose the campaign you sent (left), then tap Check the journey. Restaurants are grouped by what they did — clicked, opened, or not opened — and each group gets its own follow-up when it is due. Nothing is sent until you press Send to the list.

Anyone who bounced, complained or unsubscribed is removed automatically. So is anyone you have marked Replied, Demo booked, Trial, Won or Lost in the Venue CRM, and anyone who already received that follow-up. At most three follow-ups per campaign.
